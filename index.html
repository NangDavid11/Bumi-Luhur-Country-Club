Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know, from membership and club
access to facilities, reservations and the Bumi Luhur
experience.

01.What does a Bumi Luhur membership include?

Membership provides access to selected country club facilities, including premium swimming pool facilities, gym, studio classes, courts, entertainment and wellness
areas, as well as member-exclusive programs, special member rates and priority reservations.

02.What membership options are available?

We offer three tiers: Individual for personal pursuits, Family for parents and children, and Prestige for members who want extended privileges such as guest passes, premium lounges and personalised coaching. Each tier is available as a 6-month or 1-year membership.

03.What is the difference between the 6 Month and 1 Year Membership?

Both terms include the same benefits for your chosen tier. The 6-month term is ideal if you would like to experience the club first, while the 1-year term offers better overall value and uninterrupted access to seasonal programs and events.

04.Are there special benefits for members?

Yes. Members enjoy special rates on classes, spa treatments, dining and private events, priority booking for courts and studios, and invitations to member-only programs. Family and Prestige members receive additional privileges such as VIP or premium lounge access.

05.How do I apply for membership?

Book a tour or contact our membership team via WhatsApp. After your visit, we will guide you through choosing a tier, completing the application form and activating your membership card.

01.What are the club's operating hours?

The club is open every day. Most facilities operate from early morning until late evening, while selected areas such as the spa, restaurant and kids' spaces follow their own schedules, which are shared in the member app and at reception.

02.Can I bring guests to the club?

Yes. Members may sign in guests during operating hours. Guests must be accompanied by the hosting member, and facility fees may apply depending on your membership tier. Prestige members receive complimentary guest passes.

03.How do I access the club?

Members check in at reception with their membership card or digital pass. For security and comfort, all visitors are registered upon arrival.

04.Is there a dress code?

Smart casual attire is welcome in the clubhouse and restaurant. Appropriate sports attire and non-marking shoes are required on courts and in the fitness areas, and swimwear is limited to the pool and wellness areas.

05.Are children allowed in all areas?

Children are warmly welcome in family spaces, the pool, the playground and the Kids Club. For safety, children under the age designated by the club must be supervised by an adult, and some areas such as the gym, sauna and billiard lounge are reserved for adults.

01.Which facilities are included in my membership?

Your membership covers selected club facilities such as the swimming pool, fitness center, courts, studios, entertainment rooms and social spaces. Some premium services, including spa treatments, physiotherapy and private coaching, are offered at special member rates.

02.Are classes and personal coaching available?

Yes. We run a weekly schedule of group classes such as Pilates, functional training and swimming lessons, along with private and semi-private coaching for tennis, badminton and fitness.

03.Do you provide lockers and changing rooms?

Yes. Changing rooms with showers and lockers are available for all members, with an exclusive locker lounge for Prestige members.

04.Is parking and EV charging available?

Yes. Complimentary parking is available for members and their guests, and EV charging bays are located near the clubhouse entrance.

05.Can I use partner facilities in Kota Baru Parahyangan?

Members benefit from our integrated ecosystem, including experiences at Mason Pine Hotel, Parahyangan Golf Bandung, Bumi Pancasona and Wahoo Waterworld. Partner benefits and rates are shared by our membership team.

01.How do I reserve a court, class or treatment?

Reservations can be made through our membership team via WhatsApp or at the reception desk. Members receive priority booking for courts, studios and treatments.

02.How far in advance can I book?

Members can book in advance according to their tier, with priority windows for Family and Prestige members. We recommend booking popular time slots, such as weekend mornings, as early as possible.

03.What is the cancellation policy?

Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your reservation so the slot can be offered to other members. Late cancellations and no-shows may be subject to a fee or affect future priority booking.

04.Can I host a private event at the club?

Yes. Our Multi-Function Room, rooftop restaurant and outdoor garden can be reserved for birthdays, family celebrations and corporate gatherings. Our events team will help with menus, setup and scheduling.

05.Do I need a reservation for the restaurant?

Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend reserving a table for dinner, weekends and larger groups, especially for seats with golf course views.
